**Status:** Independent, MiMo-inspired research, not Xiaomi official design rules. Applies to original personal design projects and is not specific to SP.

## Identity

Express technical intelligence as clear, usable, human-centered content. Typography establishes structure; numbers, fine rules, and directional links organize navigation. Actual commands, data, tasks, and results are more meaningful than generic decoration. Motion explains state change or information relationship.

## Creative freedom

You may invent layouts, illustrations, visualization methods, animation treatments, and components that fit the content. Reference designs show possibilities, not allowed-only templates. Preserve legibility, semantic roles, real interaction semantics and deliberate motion. Use only the resources that are helpful to the current task.

## Boundaries

Do not claim candidate CSS, font families or durations are officially measured. Do not redistribute official Xiaomi logo, copyrighted graphics or restricted fonts. Test reduced motion and nonideal long text. Treat visual references as research inspiration rather than assets for copying.
