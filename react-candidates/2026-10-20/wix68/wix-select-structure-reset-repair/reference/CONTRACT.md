# Select22–25 source contract

The exact four originals and `IDENTITY.json` record the pinned canonical revision and SHA-256/Git blob identity. `upstream/` and `WRAPPER-IDENTITY.json` preserve the supporting Radix wrapper, label, utility, manifest and license unchanged. `CONTRACT-RESEARCH.json` is the original read-only pre-implementation research snapshot, including full option/group/default records, wrapper findings, explicit planned additions and test plan; its old `implementation_started: false` is historical, not current delivery status. `CONTRACT-RESEARCH-VERIFICATION.json` binds that research to the exact source bytes. The package README states the implemented contract and bounded differences.

22 retains two named groups with 3+2 options and no separator. 23 retains two named groups with 2+2 options and one genuine decorative separator. Supplied custom items are used exactly: separators are never inferred from the variant. 24 retains the floating associated label, adapted to allow wrapping in document flow. 25 retains a shared inset-label enclosure and borderless actual trigger, with configured popup offset 12px rather than 4px. The original wrapper also adds a translated popup transform, so matching configured offsets does not establish identical rendered edge gaps.

The originals expose parameterless examples. Controlled/uncontrolled APIs, cancellation and owner ordering, form bridge, required/reset/external form support, motion cleanup and focus repair are added behavior, not claims of exhaustive Radix parity. Actual browser/assistive-technology validation and formal acceptance remain pending.
